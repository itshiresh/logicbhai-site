Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Logic Bhai Uploader ("the app") is used only by the owner of the Logic Bhai channel to upload the channel's own videos to YouTube.

What the app accesses

The app requests the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) for the owner's own channel. It does not read, store or share any other Google account data, and it does not collect data from anyone else.

Storage and sharing

The access token is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private automation setup. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions.
